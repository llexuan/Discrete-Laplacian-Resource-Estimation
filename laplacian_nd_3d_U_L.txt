=== 3D periodic Laplacian block encoding U_L^(3) (scalable form) ===
drawn at n = 2 per dimension; <0..0|_{l,sel} U_L |0..0> = L~_p^(3).

registers:
  q_0, q_1        = block-encoding ancillas l0, l1
  q_2..q_3        = selector (K = 2)
  q_4..           = 3 system registers of n = 2 qubits

U_prep_k prepares (1/sqrt D) sum_d |d>; shifts are selector-controlled.
