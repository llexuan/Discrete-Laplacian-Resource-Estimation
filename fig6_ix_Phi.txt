=== Fig. 6 sequence (qsvt mode) ===
This is the implemented compact form of the Fig. 6 phase block.
It is operator-equivalent to the paper's decomposed projector-rotation
gadget, but the projector rotation is built directly as a 2-qubit unitary.

sequence_form: R(phi_0) W R(phi_1) W ... W R(phi_d)
  R(phi) = exp(i phi (2|Pi><Pi| - I)),  |Pi> = |+>_q |0>_a
Representative short segment (first phase + one W):
     ┌─────────┐┌────┐┌─────────┐
q_0: ┤0        ├┤0   ├┤0        ├
     │  R(phi) ││    ││  R(phi) │
q_1: ┤1        ├┤1 W ├┤1        ├
     └─────────┘│    │└─────────┘
q_2: ───────────┤2   ├───────────
                └────┘           

Full-sequence usage:
- One R(phi_k) for every QSP phase angle (118 total).
- One W between neighboring phase rotations (117 total).
- No W dagger is used in this compact repeated-walk convention.
Full-sequence usage:
- Number of phase values: 3
- Number of W applications: 2
