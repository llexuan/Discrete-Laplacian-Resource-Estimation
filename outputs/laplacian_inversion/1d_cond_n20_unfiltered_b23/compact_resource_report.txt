--- model limits and warnings ---
- Measurement-dependent corrections. The logical circuit distinguishes T from T-dagger, but the printed lattice-surgery instructions omit the gate direction and the full corrections conditioned on injection measurements. Both gates consume one magic state. Verifying the implemented operation requires checking that each measurement outcome, together with its applied or tracked correction, gives the intended gate.
- Factory timing. The runtime estimate uses the factory's average time per accepted T state across the full sequence. Even when average production meets demand, several injections close together can exhaust the stored states and delay the circuit. Quantifying that delay requires the timing of individual requests and deliveries and the capacity of the storage buffer.
