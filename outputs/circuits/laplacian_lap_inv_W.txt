=== Fig. 7-style qubitization walk W for the periodic Laplacian ===
Combined walk built from U' plus the operators X_q and U_R.

definition:
  W = U_R * X_q * U'
  U' = |0><0|_q ⊗ U_L + |1><1|_q ⊗ U_L^dag
  U_R = I - 2|Pi><Pi|, |Pi> = |+>_q |0>^(⊗3)

registers:
  q_0 = q  = qubitization control
  q_1 = l0 = block-encoding ancilla
  q_2 = l1 = block-encoding ancilla
  q_3..q_3 = k selector register (K = 1)
  remaining 2 qubits = system grid register
