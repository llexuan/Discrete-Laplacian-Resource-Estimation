=== Block encoding U_L of the scaled D-dim periodic Laplacian ===
D = 2, grid per dim N = 4; <0^3| U_L |0^3> = L~_p^(D).

registers (inside U_L):
  q_0 = l0 = block-encoding ancilla
  q_1 = l1 = block-encoding ancilla
  q_2..q_2 = k selector register (K = 1)
  next 4 qubits = D system sub-registers (n = 2 each)

sequence: [U_prep_k] ; H,Z on l0,l1 ; per-dim S^- (l1=0,k=d) & S^+ (l0=1,k=d) ; H on l0,l1 ; [U_prep_k^dag]
