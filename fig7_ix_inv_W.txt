=== Fig. 7 walk operator W (current implementation) ===
This is the signal unitary used by build_qsp_sequence.

registers:
  q_0 = q = qubitization control
  q_1 = a = block-encoding ancilla
  q_2 = s = system qubit

operator_form:
  W = (U_R \otimes I_s) S U_H'
  U_H' = |0><0|_q \otimes U_H + |1><1|_q \otimes U_H^dag
  S = X_q
  U_R = 2|+_q,0_a><+_q,0_a| - I

circuit_order:
  1. controlled U_H on q=0 branch and controlled U_H^dag on q=1 branch
  2. X on q (the shift S)
  3. decomposed reflector U_R: H_q, X_q, X_a, CZ_qa, X_q, X_a, H_q
