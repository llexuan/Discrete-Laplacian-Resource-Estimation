=== 2D periodic Laplacian block encoding U_L^(2) (scalable form) ===
drawn at n = 4 per dimension; <0..0|_{l,sel} U_L |0..0> = L~_p^(2).

registers:
  q_0, q_1        = block-encoding ancillas l0, l1
  q_2..q_2        = selector (K = 1)
  q_3..           = 2 system registers of n = 4 qubits

U_prep_k prepares (1/sqrt D) sum_d |d>; shifts are selector-controlled.
