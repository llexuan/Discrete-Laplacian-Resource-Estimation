=== 2D periodic Laplacian qubitization walk operator W ===
drawn at n = 2 per dimension (grid = 2**4); <Pi|W|Pi> = -L~_p^(2).

registers:
  q_0 = q = qubitization control
  q_1, q_2 = block-encoding ancillas l0, l1
  q_3 = dimension selector (K = 1)
  q_4.. = 2 system registers of n = 2 qubits

--- schematic form (U_L, U_L+ and U_R as opaque boxes) ---
Compact mathematical view: U' is controlled-U_L on the q=0 branch and
controlled-U_L+ on the q=1 branch, then X_q, then the reflection U_R.
